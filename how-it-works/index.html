How it works

Give important work an owner.

BizTechs are built around a specific job, your business rules, and the tools you approve—not a technical specification.

Choose the job

Hire Peter, Penny, or Garry—or describe a responsibility for a Custom BizTech.

Give the instructions

Explain what success looks like, what information the BizTech may use, and what boundaries it must follow.

Review and put it to work

Test the setup, approve how it works, and improve it based on real use.

You stay in control

Permissions

Data access and connected systems are approved during setup. BizTechs use only the tools and information you connect.

Boundaries

You define pricing rules, routing, approvals, and actions that require a human. BizTechs do not invent policy.

Flexible service

Typical setup ranges from immediate to the next business day for defined products. Service is month to month; changes are scoped as needed.
