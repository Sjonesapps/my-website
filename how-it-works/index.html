How it works

You stay in control

Permissions

Data access and connected systems are approved during setup. AI Employees use only the tools and information you connect.

Boundaries

You define pricing rules, routing, approvals, and actions that require a human. AI Employees do not invent policy.

Typical setup ranges from immediate to the next business day for defined products. Employment is monthly; changes are scoped as needed.
