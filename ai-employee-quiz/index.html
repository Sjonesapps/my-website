Red, Yellow, Green AI Bot Method

Every AI employee earns trust in three steps: Red, then Yellow, then Green.

Red

It trains first. It studies real examples. It runs practice attempts that never touch real work.

Yellow

It does real work, but out loud. Before anything goes out, it explains the plan. The manager signs off. If every item needs approval, the manager is still the bottleneck.

Green

It acts on its own, inside the limits the manager sets. It flags anything unusual for the manager to review.

Yellow builds trust. Green gives you the time back.
