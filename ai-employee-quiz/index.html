Red, Yellow, Green AI Bot Method

Every AI employee earns its way in: Red, then Yellow, then Green.

Red

It trains. It studies real examples and runs simulated attempts that never touch live work.

Yellow

It does real work out loud. Before anything goes out, it explains what it plans to do and the manager signs off. If every item waits for approval, the manager remains the throughput constraint.

Green

It acts automatically inside the authority and limits the manager sets, then surfaces fringe cases and exceptions.

Yellow builds the track record. Green is what gives you the time back.
