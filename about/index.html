About YourOS

We built BizTechs because we kept seeing the same work fall back on the owner.

Since 2017, YourOS has helped businesses make important work visible, organized, and moving. BizTechs are the practical result: specialized AI operators built around the jobs that keep getting pushed aside.

The pattern we kept finding

Growth gets buried under work nobody has time to keep moving.

Customers wait for a response. A quote gets sent and forgotten. The website needs one more update. Information sits in three systems. The owner notices all of it—and becomes the backup plan for all of it.

Your workload is ready for a clear handoff. We start with the job, then build the simplest reliable way to move it forward.

120+ operational systems

BizTechs are built from real operating experience.

We have designed more than 120 operational systems across retail, service businesses, rentals, RV, marine, HVAC, medical sampling, sales, and customer operations.

How experience becomes a BizTech

Job first. Technology second.

1. Find the workload

We identify the recurring responsibility that is costing time, opportunities, or customer trust.

2. Give it boundaries

We define the tools, rules, approvals, and handoffs the BizTech can use—and the moments that need a human.

3. Put it to work

You assign work through text, email, or phone. The BizTech handles the job and returns a useful outcome.
