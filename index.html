The Problem

Somehow, Every Job Without an Owner Becomes Yours

You probably have jobs at your company that nobody was officially hired to do.

Someone needs to answer the phone when everyone else is already helping someone else.

Someone needs to follow up after the quote is sent.

Someone needs to update the website, but it keeps getting pushed behind customer emergencies.

Someone needs to check information, move it into the right system, or make sure the customer hears back.

When nobody owns those jobs, they usually become yours. And even when you know you need help, hiring someone is not a simple decision. You have to think about cost, whether there will be enough work, whether they’ll understand the business, whether you can trust them with your customers, and whether you’ll spend weeks training them and months checking their work.

I’ve been there. I’ve hired people I later regretted hiring. I’ve worked late, gone home, and started working again. I’ve watched important work get delayed because everyone was already busy with something else.

It’s a lot. And I get it. That’s why we created BizTechs.
