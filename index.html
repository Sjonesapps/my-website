Fit check

YourOS is for owners who already know the work matters.

A BizTech is not a dashboard to babysit. It is a defined job with rules, tools, handoffs, and oversight.

Strong fit when...

  • The same work comes back every week and still depends on you.
  • Customers wait because the team is busy doing the paid work.
  • You can describe the desired outcome in plain language.
  • You want growth capacity before hiring a full-time person.

Probably not right when...

  • You want unsupported results, fake proof, or work outside approved business rules.
  • The job cannot be bounded enough to know what success looks like.
  • You need an unmanaged tool instead of a responsibility someone owns.
