Why Founding Pricing Exists

We Win When You Stay

Founding Pricing is how we earn the right to learn from you. You hire a BizTech early, before the case studies are thick and the testimonials are stacked. In exchange, you keep the rate forever. The customer who signs up next year pays more. You don't.

If you keep your subscription active, your price never changes. If you cancel and come back later, you come back at whatever pricing is current — not your old Founding rate. That's the only catch, and it's fair.

All plans: month to month, cancel anytime, with Founding pricing locked while continuously subscribed. Usage, unusual integrations, or high volume may change pricing. Garry's fee is triggered by explicit buying intent — not necessarily final collected payment.
