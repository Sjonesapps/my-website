Why Founding Salary Exists

We Win When You Stay

Founding Salary is how we earn the right to learn from you. You hire a BizTech early, before the case studies are thick and the testimonials are stacked. In exchange, you keep the rate forever. The employer who hires next year pays more. You don't.

If you keep the employee active, your salary never changes. If you cancel and come back later, you come back at whatever salary is current — not your old Founding salary. That's the only catch, and it's fair.

All employment arrangements: monthly employment, end anytime, with Founding salary locked while continuously employed. Usage, unusual integrations, or high volume may change pricing. Garry's fee is triggered by explicit buying intent — not necessarily final collected payment.
