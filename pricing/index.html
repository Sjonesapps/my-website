What the first week is—and is not

The first week is for setup and proof of fit:

  • → Clarify the responsibility and success criteria
  • → Identify the approved channels and systems
  • → Document boundaries and human-approval points
  • → Complete or test a bounded first task
  • → Review what happened and correct edge cases
  • → Decide whether the BizTech should continue, change, or stop

It is not a promise of immediate revenue growth, unlimited free work, fully autonomous decision-making, or a replacement for business judgment.

Why founding pricing exists

We earn the right to learn from you.

Founding customers hire a BizTech while the product is still being shaped by real use. In exchange for the early trust and feedback, the founding rate stays locked while that BizTech remains continuously active.

If you end the service and return later, the current rate applies. Usage, high volume, unusual integrations, and added complexity may be scoped separately. Garry's performance fee is triggered by the documented warm-lead definition above, not necessarily by final collected payment.

Availability: First 10 founding hires per public BizTech, subject to availability.

Commercial-service boundary

YourOS BizTechs are specialized commercial services delivered through AI Employees. "Hire," "salary," and "employee" describe the product experience and pricing model; they do not create a human employment relationship, payroll arrangement, benefits obligation, or authority to act outside the instructions, permissions, systems, pricing boundaries, and approval steps you authorize.

You remain responsible for reviewing the setup, granting appropriate access, approving business rules, and making decisions that require human judgment. YourOS will tell you when a job is not a good fit for a BizTech.
